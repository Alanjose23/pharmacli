Prices are the real CMS NADAC file for the week shown; this page carries a -NDC sample of that week's — every NDC you stock, every listing for the common multi-source generics, and a shallow skim of the rest. A drug's “3 here” means 3 in this sample, not 3 on the market.

Nothing here is invented. All shelf positions come from the watch table that pharmacli watch import filled from your inventory CSV — cost, on-hand and monthly volume exactly as you recorded them. A drug with no recorded volume gets no reorder timing rather than a guessed one.

Every number in the calculation panel is produced by the same functions as pharmacli reorder — move_odds, expected_price, days_to_reprice, reorder_advice — ported line for line from src/pharmacli/cli.py. Expected price is an expectation over many purchases, not a forecast of one drug's next price: right for a buying decision, wrong to quote to a patient.
